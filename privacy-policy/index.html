Privacy Policy

How your enquiry details are collected, used and stored, and how to ask about your information.

Scope and details to confirm

The legal operator’s registered details, a dedicated privacy email, staff processing countries and a retention schedule are awaiting confirmation. This notice explains the current website setup; it does not confirm legal compliance or cover unverified business practices outside the website.

Who handles your information

Goodspeed Real Estate is the name used on this website, which is aimed at Australians considering property in Thailand. The legal entity or individual responsible for the business, its registered address and registration details have not yet been provided for publication.

For a privacy question, access or correction request, deletion request or complaint, call our Australian contact on 0477 067 457 (from overseas: +61 477 067 457). A dedicated privacy email and responsible privacy contact still need to be confirmed. You can ask for this notice to be explained or provided in an accessible format.

What the website collects

The enquiry form collects your name, email address, mobile number, approximate budget in AUD, preferred location, cash or finance preference, expected buying timeframe and any optional note. All fields except the note are required to submit the form. The saved record also includes a reference, submission time and information used to recognise a repeated submission.

You can browse without submitting an enquiry, or call instead. Please provide only your own details or details you are authorised to give. Do not include passport or identity numbers, bank information, passwords, health information or other sensitive information in the note. There is no document upload, payment collection or account registration on this website.

How and why details are used

Your enquiry details are collected to record and handle your property enquiry and enable follow-up using the contact details you provide. They are not used by the website to make automated decisions about your credit, finance eligibility or suitability to buy property. Selecting “Finance required” is not a loan application or a credit check.

Submitting the form requests an enquiry response; it does not subscribe you to a newsletter or give permission for unrelated marketing. The live website currently saves enquiries and issues a receipt. Automatic email and SMS delivery to the business is not yet enabled, so a receipt does not confirm that someone has read your enquiry. Call for time-sensitive matters.

The business still needs to confirm the legal basis for processing, any later referral or marketing practices, and its procedures for handling enquiries outside the website. This notice will need to be updated if those practices are introduced.

Storage, providers and overseas processing

Submitted enquiries are stored in private storage supplied by Vercel in Singapore. Vercel also hosts the website and its enquiry service. Website requests expose technical information such as your IP address, browser information, requested page and request time to the hosting service. The enquiry service uses an IP-based request limit to help prevent abuse; the saved enquiry record does not contain your IP address.

Vercel’s hosting, support and subprocessors can involve the United States and other countries. Singapore storage does not mean that all processing occurs only in Singapore. See Vercel’s data processing information. The operator must still confirm account access, staff processing countries, provider arrangements and relevant overseas safeguards.

People granted access to the business’s hosting account can access stored enquiries. The form itself does not send your information to property sellers, developers, lenders or a mailing list. Any sharing after a call, download, referral or other offline handling is not yet confirmed and must be explained separately. An enquiry is not blanket permission to disclose your details to prospective partners.

Website storage and security

The live site uses HTTPS, private enquiry storage, input validation and request limits. There is no public enquiry directory or public account area. These safeguards reduce some risks; no online service can guarantee absolute security. Access control reviews, backup arrangements and incident response procedures still require confirmation by the operator.

Unsubmitted details are held in the current page’s memory; the website does not save an unfinished enquiry to the server or browser storage. Reloading or closing the page resets the form. Your browser may independently remember entries or restore a page under its own settings. Fonts and images are served by this website. We have not added analytics, advertising pixels or social-media tracking. Read the Cookies & Website Storage notice for more information.

Retention and deletion

Stored enquiries do not currently expire automatically. They remain in storage until someone with the necessary access removes them. The business has not yet confirmed a retention schedule, review interval, legal recordkeeping requirements or the retention of downloaded records, hosting logs and backups. This is an outstanding operational requirement, not a promise to keep information indefinitely.

You can request deletion using the contact above. A request needs to be reviewed against any applicable legal recordkeeping duty, dispute or other lawful reason to retain information. Removing a website record does not automatically remove a copy that has been downloaded or held by a provider; those locations must also be checked. We cannot presently promise a specific deletion date or backup expiry period.

Your choices, requests and complaints

You can ask what personal information is held about you, request access or a copy, ask for correction, request deletion, or ask to stop enquiry follow-up. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, portability, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis. These rights can be subject to legal exceptions.

Call the Australian contact and explain your request. If available, give your enquiry reference. Only information reasonably needed to locate your record and verify your authority should be requested; do not send identity documents through the enquiry form. Privacy requests need to be checked, recorded and answered under the applicable legal requirements. The operator has not yet confirmed a dedicated request-handling contact or service timeframe.

For a complaint, explain what happened and the outcome you seek. You may also be able to contact the relevant privacy regulator, such as the Australian Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or the Philippine National Privacy Commission, where they have jurisdiction. The applicable regulator depends on the business and processing locations.

Scope and updates

This notice concerns the public website and its current enquiry form. Phone, email, in-person services, property transactions and any later customer systems may involve different information and need their own notices. The website is intended for adults planning a property purchase; please contact us if a child’s information has been submitted.

Australian, Thai and Philippine privacy-law applicability needs review once the operator and processing locations are confirmed. No claim is made that this notice meets every requirement of those laws. Material changes to website data handling should be reflected here and explained before a new use begins.
