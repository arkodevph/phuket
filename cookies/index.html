Cookies & Website Storage

What this website keeps in your browser, which services it loads, and how to control browser storage.

Last reviewed: 11 October 2026

On this page

Scope and details to confirm

This notice describes the public website’s current configuration. New analytics, advertising, account or embedded services would need a fresh privacy and cookie assessment before they are added.

Cookies and tracking

We have not added analytics cookies, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or social-media embeds to this website. There is no cookie-based account sign-in or advertising preference profile. The website does not use browser local storage or session storage to save enquiries.

The hosting provider, Vercel, may use technical mechanisms for security or operating the service. Provider infrastructure and protected deployment previews can differ from the public website. This notice does not promise that a provider will never use a technical cookie. Any newly introduced visitor tracking must be assessed and disclosed before activation.

Your enquiry draft

Before you submit, the details you type, the review screen and a submission reference used to recognise a retry are kept in the current page’s memory. Reloading or closing the page resets the form; the website does not save your unfinished enquiry to the server or browser storage. Your browser or password manager may separately remember entries or restore a page under its own settings.

When you send an enquiry, the details are stored on the server and a receipt is shown. Clearing your browser’s cookies or browsing history does not delete a submitted enquiry. See the Privacy Policy’s retention and deletion section to request removal.

Fonts, images and technical requests

The fonts and images used by the public website are hosted with the website. Fonts are not requested from Google Fonts when you visit. Your browser may cache these files to load the site faster; that cache is different from storing your submitted enquiry.

Vercel hosts the website and handles technical information associated with web requests, including IP addresses and browser information. Submitted enquiry data is stored in Singapore, while hosting and support can involve other countries. Read the Privacy Policy’s storage section for details and outstanding confirmations.

Your controls and future changes

You can clear or block cookies, site data and cached files through your browser settings. Restricting JavaScript stops the interactive enquiry form, but these legal pages remain readable. You can use the Australian contact number 0477 067 457 instead.

There is currently no non-essential tracking service for a cookie banner to enable or disable. This does not settle every jurisdiction’s cookie requirements. If tracking, embedded maps, videos, chat, analytics or accounts are added, the operator must check the applicable consent requirements and provide suitable information and controls before those services load.
