# Questions for the Goodspeed Real Estate client

Prepared on 11 October 2026. This is a draft to send to the client; it has not been sent. The public website notices describe the verified website setup and explicitly flag the unanswered details. Do not substitute guesses for the client's answers.

## Ready-to-send message

Hi,

We’re preparing the privacy, website terms and property ownership notices for the Goodspeed Real Estate website. Please help us confirm the following so that the pages reflect your business accurately. If an answer is not available yet, “to confirm” is fine.

1. **Who legally operates Goodspeed Real Estate?** Please provide the full company name or sole trader/owner’s legal name, registration country, registered or business address, and relevant registration numbers. Is Goodspeed Real Estate a registered business name?
2. **Where do you operate and handle customer enquiries?** Which countries are you, your staff and any contractors based in when they can access enquiry details? In particular, are any enquiries handled in Australia, Thailand or the Philippines? Which countries’ residents will you actively serve?
3. **Who should handle privacy requests and complaints?** Please confirm the responsible person, a monitored privacy email, and whether **0477 067 457 / +61 477 067 457** can be published for privacy enquiries as well as property enquiries. What should happen when someone asks for a copy, correction or deletion of their information?
4. **What happens after a website enquiry?** Will details be downloaded, emailed, texted, stored in a CRM or spreadsheet, or passed to agents, sellers, developers, lawyers, lenders or referral partners? Please name the providers/recipients and explain the purpose. Will you send marketing separately from responding to an enquiry?
5. **How long should enquiry records be kept?** Please confirm periods for unsuccessful enquiries, ongoing clients, completed transactions, exports, emails, SMS records and backups, including any legal recordkeeping duties. Who will review and remove records? The website currently has no automatic expiry.
6. **Who controls the hosting and its security?** Who has access to the Vercel account and stored enquiries? Are multi-factor authentication, access reviews, backups, provider data-processing terms and an incident-response contact in place? Enquiries are stored in Singapore; hosting/support may involve other countries. Can you confirm the arrangements for overseas handling?
7. **What is your commercial role?** Are you a buyer adviser, licensed property agent, introducer, developer representative or another type of business? Please provide any required licences, the parties you represent, fees/commissions/referral payments, and existing client agreements. Do you provide finance advice or only refer to a separate licensed provider?
8. **Are there existing legal documents or compliance arrangements?** Please share any lawyer-approved privacy policy, service terms, retention rules or data-protection arrangements. For applicability checks, please indicate whether annual business turnover exceeds A$3 million, whether you have at least 250 staff, and whether you process sensitive information about at least 1,000 people. These answers help a lawyer assess coverage and registration duties; they do not determine compliance on their own.
9. **Can the website content be verified?** Please provide authorised property listings, evidence of availability/prices and foreign freehold quota where relevant, rights to images, and any genuine testimonials with permission. The current site has area guides and illustrative images, rather than verified sale listings. Please also confirm whether you plan accounts, payments, maps, chat, analytics or document uploads.

Once we have these details, we can update the notices and ask a qualified legal professional to review the relevant Australian, Thai and, if applicable, Philippine requirements.

Thank you.

## Priorities for the website owner

The highest-priority answers are questions 1–6: legal identity/address, processing locations, privacy contact, sharing, retention and hosting/access arrangements. A published policy cannot resolve missing operational arrangements. No email recipient or notification provider has been invented or activated.
