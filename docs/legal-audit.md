## Applicable-law assessment

These are issues for the operator and a qualified professional to determine, rather than conclusions inferred from the developer’s location.

- **Australia:** The audience and Australian phone make an Australian business-link and consumer-law assessment relevant. Privacy Act coverage depends on turnover, exemptions and exceptions, and whether the operator carries on business in Australia. If covered, the APPs address policy transparency, collection notices, appropriate use, overseas disclosures, safeguards and retention, access and correction. The small-business exemption is not automatic proof of exclusion. Check Australian Consumer Law obligations and any applicable property/credit licensing before making commercial claims. Sources: [OAIC coverage and policy guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/your-privacy-rights/your-personal-information/what-is-a-privacy-policy), [APP 5 collection notices](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-5-app-5-notification-of-the-collection-of-personal-information), [APP 8 overseas disclosure](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-8-app-8-cross-border-disclosure-of-personal-information), [APP 11 security and retention](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information), [ACCC real estate](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/specific-products-and-activities/real-estate), [ACCC consumer guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees).
- **Philippines:** RA 10173 and its IRR require an applicability assessment if the operator, processing equipment, staff, contractors or other relevant links involve the Philippines. Sections 4 and 6 describe scope and extraterritorial links; a Manila developer timezone alone is insufficient to establish the business’s position. If applicable, assess lawful grounds, proportionality, transparency, rights, security, accountable transfers, DPO designation and any required DPS/DPO registration. Do not assume all businesses must register: NPC Circular 2022-04 includes thresholds and risk-based criteria. Sources: [NPC official Data Privacy Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/), [IRR](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/), [NPC Circular 2022-04](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04.pdf).
- **Thailand:** A Thai establishment or qualifying offering of services to people in Thailand can make the Thai PDPA relevant; merely showing Thai destinations does not establish every applicability condition. Review territorial scope, notice/lawful grounds, transfers, security, retention, rights and conditional officer/representative duties once operations are known. Source: [MDES English PDPA translation](https://www.mdes.go.th/uploads/tinymce/source/%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%AA/Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202019.pdf), notably sections 5, 23, 24, 28, 37–41.
- **Other markets:** No evidence of deliberate EU/UK targeting was found. Reassess other jurisdictions if the client adds countries, resident users or monitoring. Do not publish a blanket GDPR or global-compliance claim.

The property ownership notice uses the generally applicable foreign condominium quota of **49% by aggregate floor area, not unit count**, and distinguishes condominium freehold from land and leasehold. It does not assess any buyer’s eligibility or a specific property. Official background: [Thai government transfer-document guidance](https://www.thailand.go.th/public/index.php/guide-book-detail/010_011), [foreign property ownership overview](https://www.thailand.go.th/public/issue-focus-detail/006_004). General guidance is not a substitute for current Land Office and independent Thai legal verification.

## Implemented website notices

| Page or notice | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `/privacy-policy/` | Actual enquiry fields and metadata, purposes, current notification limitation, provider/overseas handling, safeguards, retention/deletion limitations, access/correction/complaints and missing business facts. |
| `/terms-and-conditions/` | Website scope, enquiries, illustrative content, independent advice, use of the service and non-excludable consumer rights. No invented governing law, licences, fees, arbitration or liability waiver. |
| `/cookies/` | Actual browser memory/cache use and absence of application-added analytics/advertising storage, provider caveats and reassessment before new tracking. |
| `/property-disclaimer/` | Area-guide status, foreign condominium freehold/quota, land/leasehold distinctions, currency/price cautions, advice and no guarantees of returns/finance/visas. |
| Form collection notice | Shown while entering details and immediately before sending; explains purpose, Singapore storage and unresolved operator/retention facts; Privacy/Terms open separately without discarding the draft. Optional note warns against sensitive information. |

All legal pages are static HTML, accessible without JavaScript, with the existing typefaces/colours, a table of contents, contact details and shared cross-links. Homepage footer and relevant property sections link to them. Vite produces all five HTML entries; local Express and Vercel have explicit handling for legal routes. Public fonts are self-hosted with their SIL OFL licences, removing visitor requests to Google Fonts. The placeholder testimonial was replaced with an editorial statement and unsupported investment/management claims were softened.

A cookie consent banner was not added: no non-essential tracking integration exists to control. This is a finding about the current implementation, not a legal exemption for future features. Ecommerce refund/checkout, public account/registration and upload notices are not implemented because those functions do not exist. Reassess if introduced. These website terms do not replace agency/service agreements, finance disclosures, referral/commission disclosures, AML obligations or transaction documents if applicable to the actual business.

## Remaining gaps and required decisions

1. **Identify the operator.** Obtain legal name, business/registered address, registration details, role and a monitored privacy contact; confirm the public phone. Without the operator’s identity, the privacy notice cannot be treated as complete where controller-identification duties apply.
2. **Determine law and lawful grounds.** Confirm operating/access countries, target residents, Australian coverage/exceptions, Thai scope and Philippine links. A lawyer must assess processing grounds, notices, transfer arrangements, licensing and any officer/registration duties.
3. **Define and enforce retention.** Records still have no automatic expiry. The client must approve a justified schedule, legal holds, manual review/deletion responsibility and treatment of exports, queues, email/SMS, logs and backups. Transparently disclosing this gap does not cure an excessive-retention practice.
4. **Adopt privacy operations.** The [operations checklist](privacy-operations.md) is a proposed runbook, not evidence that the operator has staffed or adopted it. Confirm identity checks, secure response delivery, deadlines, incident escalation, access control and provider contracts.
5. **Review overseas handling and sharing.** Singapore storage is confirmed; all staff/subprocessor locations, contractual safeguards, actual account permissions and offline/referral sharing remain unknown. Review [Vercel’s published DPA](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa) and actual account arrangements. No signed DPA or full provider-security audit has been verified.
6. **Review before activating email/SMS or new features.** Confirm recipient address, providers/countries, access and retention, update notices and assess any required consent. The local queue can replay old pending data. Do not treat enquiry follow-up as consent to unrelated marketing or third-party lead distribution.
7. **Verify commercial content.** Actual listings, prices, imagery rights, ownership claims, licences, fees/commissions and genuine testimonials require evidence. The earlier request for verified listings and wider location coverage is not completed by legal-page work.

Use the [client questionnaire](client-legal-questionnaire.md) to obtain these facts. Have a qualified professional review the final policies and business operations. Legal pages alone do not guarantee compliance or eliminate fines/claims.

## Verification

- `npm test`: **12 passing checks**, including validation, persistence, retries, rollback, rate limits, mocked notification handling and the new direct legal-document routing regression check.
- `npm run build`: **passed**, producing the homepage and four separate HTML documents, stylesheets and local font assets.
- Chromium verification against the built local Express app: **passed** for all legal documents at widths 320, 390, 768 and 1440px; no horizontal overflow, browser errors or failed asset requests. Reviewed desktop/mobile privacy-page and form screenshots.
- Legal page titles, table-of-contents targets, footer links, cross-document fragment links and direct routes with/without trailing slashes were checked. All four documents remain readable with JavaScript disabled.
- Opening Privacy from the form preserved the draft; the collection notice remained visible on review. A simulated submission failure retained details; a subsequent synthetic submission saved the expected details in a temporary local SQLite database and returned the displayed reference. No real email/SMS was sent.
- Original public-site inspection found Google Fonts hosts and no cookies in the tested session. Updated local inspection found only website-origin requests, loaded all three font families, and found no cookies. This is an observation of tested sessions, not a promise that infrastructure can never set a technical cookie.
- Production verification is recorded after deployment. No real enquiry records or client recipients were used for testing; no existing data was accessed, altered or deleted.
